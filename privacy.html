Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, travel dates, group details, budget, interests, and the message you provide.

How we use it

We use this information to respond to enquiries, design travel plans, provide requested services, maintain records, and improve our service. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and security

Enquiries are stored in an access-controlled database. We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, booking, legal, and accounting purposes.

Privacy-friendly analytics

We count aggregate page views to understand which pages are useful. This first-party counter does not use advertising cookies, does not create a visitor profile, and does not store your IP address. Browser Do Not Track signals are respected.

Sharing

We may share necessary trip information with selected accommodation, transport, guide, and activity partners when arranging your journey, or when required by law.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete your information by emailing tuskersedgesafari@gmail.com.
